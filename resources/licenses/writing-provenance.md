# Writing resources

The nine unchanged Ziya prompts in Chinese Writing 1.1 come from [Ziya-Writing-Eval-Chinese](https://huggingface.co/datasets/IDEA-CCNL/Ziya-Writing-Eval-Chinese) at revision `f55b23a942608083cffe2813c803d3517ebb1e41`, under Apache-2.0. See the bundled dataset card and Apache-2.0 license.

`dsh-original:news-materials-01`, `dsh-original:meeting-actions-01` and `dsh-original:community-fund-report-01` are project-authored fictional prompts and evaluation checklists under this project's MIT license. Each prompt's SHA-256 is recorded beside its source ID. Chinese Writing 1.1 replaces `ziya:13` and retains the other eleven questions and their scoring criteria.

The exact Chinese Writing 1.0 data remains in `protocols/writing-v2.json` for historical reports and resuming version 1.0 runs. Earlier WritingBench prompts are available only from an intact prior local cache.
